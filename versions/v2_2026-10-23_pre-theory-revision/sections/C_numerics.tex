\section{Numerical methods and verification}\label{app:numerics}

\paragraph{Solving the researcher's problem.} For each $P>0$ the first-order
condition $\partial_r\ln g(r,P)=0$ has a unique root in $[0,1)$ because $\ln g$ is
strictly concave in $r$. We find it by vectorised bisection (60 steps)
followed by three Newton steps, and set $r^*=0$ when $\partial_r\ln g(0,P)\le0$.
The derivative $v'/v$ is computed from the envelope formula
\eqref{eq:envelope}, not by differencing. Peaks and crossings use bounded
Brent searches on the log-concave curves.

\paragraph{Stationary equilibrium and planner.} The equilibrium is the solution of
$\ln w(P)=\ln F(M(P))$ on $[\hat P_w,\infty)$. Here $M(P)$ solves
$\Phi(M)+Mn(P)=N$, and the left side minus the right side is strictly decreasing
(proof of \cref{prop:eq}). The constrained planner maximises output over $P$ along the
resource constraint. The full planner maximises over $(P,x)$ by Nelder--Mead.

\paragraph{Forward-looking entry.} The value $U(P)$ is computed by the trapezoid
rule on 400 nodes of the consolidation path plus the closed-form tail at $P^m$.
The stopping density is the first root of $\ln U(P)-\kappa\delta P/(\delta+g)=\ln F$
above $P^m$, bracketed on a log grid and refined by Brent's method.

\paragraph{Life cycles.} The growth phase has closed forms,
$P=P_0e^{gt}$ and $S=P(1-e^{-(g+\delta)t})/(g+\delta)$. The stopping time is found
on a fine time grid and refined by linear interpolation. Consolidation and
maturity follow \cref{prop:toofast}(iii). Diffusion rates and fertility are lognormal, truncated at $\pm2.5$ log
standard deviations and integrated on evenly spaced nodes. Because mature
density is very elastic with respect to fertility ($1/|\eps_w|\approx7$),
untruncated tails, where a few extremely fertile topics carry many
researchers, make the moments converge very slowly. Topic-years are weighted by members, $P/x^*(P)$, and by topic
survival, $e^{-\xi t}$. Observed log density adds classical measurement error
with a standard deviation of 0.3, integrated by seven-point Gauss--Hermite
quadrature. Besides matching the noisy arXiv proxy, this makes the quintile
moments smooth in the parameters. The search uses a coarse grid (15 diffusion
rates, 15 fertility levels, $\Delta t=0.1$). Candidates are re-ranked, and all
reported moments computed, on a fine grid (41 diffusion rates, 51 fertility
levels, $\Delta t=0.05$). Across grids the gradient and the share beyond the
peak move by less than half a percentage point. The quintile moments move by
up to one point, because the cut points between fifths fall in different places.
The search bounds the median diffusion rate of new topics at 0.5 a year, so that
the median topic takes at least about three and a half years to mature. With the
bound at 1, both the baseline and the within-topic searches end in corners in
which the median rate hits the bound, almost every topic overshoots, and the
learning efficiency, catch-up speed and fertility dispersion sit at their lower
bounds. The corners' losses (16.5 baseline, 12.0 within-topic) are higher than
those of the interior solutions we report (14.9 and 11.4).

\paragraph{Verification.} The file \texttt{code/tests/test\_propositions.py}
checks every analytical result numerically:
\begin{itemize}
\item log-concavity, single-peakedness, $v(0)=1$ and $v\to0$ on 25 random parameter draws satisfying \cref{ass:Q,ass:phi,ass:credit,ass:interior};
\item the envelope formula against finite differences, and the monotonicity of $r^*(P)$ (\cref{thm:v});
\item the initial slope $v'(0^+)=\sup_\varrho\nu_0(\varrho)$ (\cref{ass:interior}(b));
\item the externality decomposition and its sign (\cref{prop:externality});
\item existence, uniqueness and the falling-side location of equilibrium for several $(\kappa,\eta)$, and dissipation (\cref{prop:eq});
\item the volume elasticity \eqref{eq:E} against finite differences, and exact invariance to $N$ and $a$ when $\eta=0$ (\cref{prop:volume});
\item the planner's first-order condition, the ordering $\hat P_w<P^o<P^*$, the large-topic limit and the vanishing writing wedge (\cref{prop:planner});
\item the cross-topic returns gradient $\kappa P^m$ (\cref{prop:mature}) and overcrowding with rising returns (\cref{cor:overcrowd});
\item the monotonicity and limits of the myopic stopping density, the threshold $g^*$, and $P_c\ge P_c^\infty(g)$ for finite birth flows (\cref{prop:toofast}(i));
\item under forward-looking entry, the ordering $g^*\le g^*_{RE}\le\delta(\Phat/P^m-1)$, overshooting above the bound and none below $g^*$, deeper overshooting than under myopia for fast topics, and stopping beyond the frictionless density as $g\to\infty$ (\cref{prop:toofast}(ii)--(iii));
\item the social-value identities and the overcrowding condition under transfers (\cref{prop:social});
\item the composition bias of the short-run volume elasticity against its covariance formula (\cref{rem:shortrun});
\item dissipation of reading improvements when $\eta=0$, and, when $\eta>0$, the sign of $\dd\ln(Y/N)/\dd\omega=-\eta\,\dd\ln M^*/\dd\omega>0$ (\cref{prop:solow});
\item the stepping-on-toes formula \eqref{eq:sot} against finite differences (\cref{prop:sot});
\item a Monte Carlo check of the credit formula \eqref{eq:credit} (\cref{lem:credit}).
\end{itemize}
All checks pass.
